APPLICATION TIPS

Quick wins for your profile

Tap a topic to see more.

Write authentically about who you are, what has shaped you, and why a college fits your goals. Specific details and your own voice usually matter more than trying to sound overly formal.
Check whether your colleges are test-optional and compare your scores with their typical admitted student ranges. If you decide to retake the SAT or ACT, give yourself enough time to prepare.
Depth can be more meaningful than joining everything. Long-term commitment, leadership, improvement, and genuine interests can help show colleges what matters to you.
An upward academic trend can show growth and persistence. Keep challenging yourself at an appropriate level while maintaining strong performance.
Ask teachers or counselors who genuinely know your work and character. Give them plenty of notice and share helpful context about your goals.
Break applications into smaller tasks instead of waiting until the deadline. Starting early gives you more time to revise essays, request recommendations, and double-check requirements.
