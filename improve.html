APPLICATION TIPS

Quick wins for your profile

Tap a topic to see more.

Write about experiences that genuinely matter to you. The goal isn't to sound like a robot with a thesaurus — let your personality, growth, and perspective come through.
Give yourself enough time to prepare and consider taking the SAT or ACT more than once if it makes sense for your goals. Track your attempts here so you can see your progress.
Depth can matter more than having a giant list. Long-term involvement, leadership, initiative, and real impact can help show what you're actually passionate about.
Colleges can look at your academic record over time. An upward trend can help demonstrate growth, especially when your course rigor and overall circumstances are considered too.
Ask teachers or mentors who actually know you well and can speak about your work ethic, character, curiosity, or growth with specific examples.
Break applications into smaller steps before deadlines get scary. Essays, recommendation requests, forms, and financial aid stuff become way less chaotic when you aren't doing everything at 11:59 PM.
