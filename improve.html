APPLICATION TIPS

Quick wins for your profile

Strong Essays
Write authentically about who you are and why each school fits you. Admissions officers read thousands — stand out by being genuine.
Test Strategy
Take the SAT/ACT multiple times. Most schools consider your highest score, and retaking often raises results.
Extracurriculars
Depth beats breadth. Schools value meaningful commitment to a few activities over a long list of minimal involvement.
GPA Trends
If your GPA improved over time, that shows growth. Colleges see the full picture, not just the final number.
Letters of Rec
Ask teachers who know you well and can speak to your character, not just your grades.
Start Early
Apply early (September/October). It reduces stress and shows genuine interest to schools that track application timing.
