Why I built this

NJ

I'm a first-generation college applicant in the US. My parents went to school in a different country, so a lot of how American college admissions and financial aid actually work wasn't something I could just ask them about at dinner. I had to figure it out on my own, and honestly, seeing those huge sticker prices for the first time was pretty overwhelming.

I built CapItNJ to make this whole process a little easier for other students and parents going through the same thing. The idea is simple. Put your information in once, and get back a realistic list of schools that actually fit your grades and your family's budget, without all the confusing extra steps.

It's still a work in progress, built by a student in NJ for families in a similar spot. Fewer surprises, simpler steps, and a clearer path forward.

How it helps

One profile. A realistic shortlist.

Instead of scrolling through endless ranking lists, CapItNJ looks at real numbers to show you options that actually fit what you're looking for.

Cost, not sticker price

Matches are based on the estimated net price for your income tier, not the scary number on a school's website.

Your academic fit

Your GPA gets weighed against each school's typical admit, so you can clearly see your reach, match, and safety schools.

Local to global

Results are grouped into simple categories based on location and how far you're willing to go.
