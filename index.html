Why I built this

NJ-06

I'm a first-generation college applicant in the US. My parents went to university in a different country, so a lot of how American college admissions and financial aid work wasn't something I could just ask them about at dinner. I had to piece it together myself, and looking at the giant sticker prices was genuinely overwhelming.

I built CapItNJ for the Congressional App Challenge to make this process easier for other students and parents. The goal is simple: put your information in once, and get a realistic list of options that actually fit your grades and family budget without confusing complications.

It's a work in progress built by an NJ student for families in similar positions—fewer surprises, simpler steps, and a clearer guide.
